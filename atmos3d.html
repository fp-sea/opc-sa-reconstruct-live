OPC North Pacific — 3D Atmosphere Explorer

Educational visualization of raw model forecast output (not an official product, not for navigation). Model data: NOAA GFS; ECMWF IFS open data © ECMWF, CC BY 4.0. Fields are drawn at their true height with vertical exaggeration; frames between the model's real 3-hourly steps are blended for smooth motion and are labelled as such.
